Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

Disclaimer: This is a template. Before going live, have a lawyer review — especially if you serve EU (GDPR), California (CCPA), or enterprise customers.

1. Information we collect

When you use Panelist, we collect:

  • Account information — name, email, company, role.
  • Interview data — job descriptions, rubrics, candidate names (if provided), scorecards, and video/audio recordings.
  • Usage data — pages visited, features used, device / browser info.
  • Communications — messages you send us via forms, email, or chat.

2. How we use your information

  • To operate and improve the Panelist platform.
  • To match your role with appropriate interviewers.
  • To send transactional emails (confirmations, scorecards, invoices).
  • To send occasional product updates (you can unsubscribe anytime).
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. How we share information

We share information only with:

  • Interviewers assigned to your role (JD + rubric only, not candidate PII unless you provide it).
  • Service providers like our hosting, email, and payment vendors under strict data-processing agreements.
  • Legal authorities when required by law.

We never sell your data.

4. Data security

We use industry-standard security including TLS in transit, encryption at rest, access controls, and regular audits. No system is 100% secure, but we work hard to protect your data.

5. Your rights

Depending on your region, you have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data. Email privacy@panelist.io to make a request.

6. Cookies

We use essential cookies for authentication and analytics cookies to understand product usage. You can control cookies via your browser settings.

7. Changes to this policy

We'll notify you of material changes via email or in-app notice.

8. Contact

Questions? Email privacy@panelist.io.
