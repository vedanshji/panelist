FAQ

Questions, answered.

How do you vet interviewers?
Every panelist passes a 3-stage vetting process: credential verification, a mock interview against our internal rubric, and a calibration session with a senior Panelist reviewer. We accept less than 8% of applicants.
What roles do you cover?
Engineering (all stacks), product, design, data science, sales, marketing, operations, and executive search. If we don't have coverage for a niche role, we'll source and calibrate a specialist within 5 business days.
How is this different from Karat or Interviewing.io?
Panelist is multi-domain (not just engineering), offers deeper calibration to your team's specific rubric, and delivers structured evidence over binary pass/fail. We also cover senior and executive roles that most competitors don't.
How do you handle NDAs and IP?
Every engagement is covered by a per-interview NDA and conflict-of-interest check. Interviewers cannot work with direct competitors during an active engagement window.
Can we bring our own rubric?
Absolutely — Growth and Enterprise plans include a calibration session where our team learns your rubric, your team's "look-fors," and past hiring bar. New panelists shadow live calibrated interviews before flying solo on your account.
