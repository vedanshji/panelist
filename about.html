Our story

Great teams deserve
great signal.

We started Panelist because hiring is broken — engineers hate running screens, candidates hate inconsistent bars, and companies pay the tax in bad hires and slow pipelines. We think there's a better way.

The problem we're solving

Every fast-growing company hits the same wall: senior engineers spending 20–30% of their week running first-round interviews. Product cycles slip. Interview quality drifts. Onsite conversion drops. And your best people burn out.

Meanwhile, thousands of senior operators — engineers, PMs, designers, sales leaders — would love to earn extra income doing what they already do well: evaluating talent.

What we built

Panelist is the marketplace and platform that connects the two. We take a JD, match it to a vetted domain expert, deliver a structured interview against your rubric, and hand back a scorecard your team can trust — all in under 24 hours.

No more "vibes-based" pass/fail. No more engineers pulled off roadmap to run 5 screens a week. Just calibrated signal, on demand.

Our values

  • Signal > speed. We'd rather ship one carefully scored interview than five sloppy ones.
  • Interviewers first. Our panel is our product. We pay well, train continuously, and protect their time.
  • Evidence over opinion. Every scorecard cites specific candidate responses.
  • Bias is our enemy. Structured rubrics + calibration = fairer outcomes.
